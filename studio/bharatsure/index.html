Overview

A four-year design partnership that scaled a benefits-tech startup from concept to ecosystem, while nurturing a product team into design-led thinkers.

Bharatsure set out to make health insurance and employee benefits more accessible for India’s corporate employees. They needed a long-term design partner who could deliver with conviction and, more importantly, bring clarity to the way product managers thought and decisions were made.

What we did

We came on board at the very beginning, when the idea was still a sketch, and shaped the product through every phase: MVP, scale and evolution. But beyond screens and flows, our impact lay in helping the team adopt a design mindset, one that now guides how they frame problems, draft PRDs and collaborate across sprints.

  • End-to-end product design
  • Design system creation and scaling
  • Prototyping and UX strategy
  • Product mentorship and mindset building

Today we work closely with founders, product managers and developers as a design partner and coach. The engagement has run for over four years, and continues.

The challenge

Our biggest challenge was our users: mostly independent insurance brokers, experts in insurance but not in digital workflows. They were used to pen, paper and WhatsApp. Their needs were real, but their habits, and little motivation to learn a new platform, meant design had to do more than enable tasks. It had to educate and uplift.

Building foundations first

Robust foundational modules came first. They gave everyone a clean layer of understanding before we built transactional features and workflows, like the members and claims modules.

A seamless ecosystem

The product today is a complex, integrated platform. From member onboarding to claims and analytics, we designed each module to feel intuitive, cohesive and scalable.

“What we’re most proud of isn’t just the product. It’s the mindset shift. Growing with the team as they adopted design as a way of thinking, not just an output, is the kind of impact you can’t wireframe.”

Vasu Agrawal, Design Lead

Scaling with a design system

We built and maintained a comprehensive design system in Figma. As Healthysure grew and evolved into Bharatsure, and its mobile and tablet interfaces grew with it, the system scaled too. Everything was documented in detail, so designers and developers could onboard quickly and stay consistent, even as teams changed.
