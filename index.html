About

I started out making physical products. As an industrial designer I sketched, modelled and prototyped off-screen, and the habit of making things real early has stayed with me ever since.

I was the founding industrial designer at GreyOrange, shaping warehouse automation from the first sketches, and went on to design products and packaging at Ticket Design. At SapientRazorfish I crossed over from objects to phygital experiences across mixed reality surfaces: smart tables, intelligent mirrors and a patented assistive braille reader for the visually impaired.

More than six years at frog came next. As Interaction Design Lead I worked on multidisciplinary engagements for Fortune 500 clients, and learned how design earns its seat at the table.

In 2024 I started studio baagh, working across domains to give founders and executives the creative leadership and product thinking that keeps them ahead of the curve on a constantly evolving global canvas.
